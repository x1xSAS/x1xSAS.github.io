Journal Publications

HCFFormer: A Hybrid CNN–Transformer Framework for Heterogeneous Remote Sensing Image Change Detection

Salman Alsalman and Amina Salhi
International Journal of Remote Sensing, 2026.

DOI: 10.1080/01431161.2026.2691979

This work proposes HCFFormer, a hybrid CNN–Transformer framework for heterogeneous remote sensing image change detection. The study reflects my current research direction in deep learning, computer vision, remote sensing, and intelligent image analysis.
